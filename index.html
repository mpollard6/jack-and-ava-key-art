Proposed

Both at the exact size Tubi renders a browse tile. The current art is the release poster, which puts a cream border and a boxed still into a slot that would give you every pixel of it.

Tubi's grid is portrait, so the 16:9 cover has no slot here and this needs its own asset. The proposed tile is the same frame and the same logotype as Option 1, recomposed to fill 408×583 edge to edge, and it clears both chins rather than just both mouths. The empty wall between the two of them has also been compressed, which closes the gap from 41 percent of the tile down to 16 and brings noticeably more of both faces into frame. The compression is confined strictly to that wall: both faces come through as byte-identical copies, translated but never resampled.
